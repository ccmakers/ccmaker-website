Have you been waiting for the right moment to turn your sketches into something real? This is it.

Join Cape Cod Makers as a **Supporting Member** and bridge the gap between "idea" and "finished project." Whether you want to master a specific machine or simply don't know where to start, we're here to help.

## What You Get

For **$120/year**, a Supporting Membership gives you direct access to powerful digital fabrication tools and a community of experienced makers who want to see you succeed.

Our veteran members will walk you through our most popular tools:

- **3D Printing** — Learn the path from digital model to physical object. We'll use UltiMaker Cura to slice a model and run a print, covering the exact settings that lead to reliable results.
- **Laser Cutting with Glowforge** — Go from a digital design to a finished piece in minutes. We'll use the Glowforge app to design and cut a custom project in wood or acrylic.
- **CNC Carving** — Get hands-on with the X-Carve and Easel software. We'll walk through setup and run a simple carve so you can see the machine in action.
- **Vinyl & Papercraft** — Master the Silhouette Cameo Pro for cutting vinyl, cardstock, and fabric — from custom decals to textile arts.

## How It Works

1. **Join** — Sign up as a Supporting Member.
2. **Get trained** — We'll pair you with a veteran maker for a one-on-one session during our regular member hours, so you get focused attention right away. Group workshops are also scheduled when enough new members are ready to kick one off.
3. **Start creating** — With the access and support in place, you're ready to build anything you can imagine.

Ready to get started? Email us at [info@capecodmakers.org](mailto:info@capecodmakers.org) to join the community today.
